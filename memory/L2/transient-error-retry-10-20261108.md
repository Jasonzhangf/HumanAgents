# Ontology: Automatic backoff retry up to 10 times for transient errors

Transient errors (such as sporadic 500s) must automatically retry with backoff up to 10 times while preserving the exact same Task semantics and context. Only after 10 retries still fail is the error explicitly exposed to the user.
